Our Studio

A decade of quiet luxury, made in Mumbai.

We are a collective of designers, carpenters, and craftspeople united by a single obsession — interiors that age beautifully.

Our Story

From a two-person workshop to Mumbai's boutique studio.

Aurelia began in 2014 in a modest Bandra workshop — one designer, one carpenter, and a shared belief that Indian interiors deserved global standards without losing their soul.

Today, we've grown into a 42-member studio, delivering 500+ residences and offices, yet every project still passes through our founders' hands. Scale, without compromise.

Our Principles

Three pillars that hold up every project.

Mission

To design interiors that feel considered, crafted, and permanent — spaces that our clients' grandchildren will still love.

Vision

To become India's most trusted boutique interior atelier — where design integrity outranks trend, always.

Our Promise

Honest quotations, master craftsmanship, on-time delivery, and a 10-year structural warranty. In writing.
